# SportFinder: Your Movement Matchmaker

## The Vision

**SportFinder** is an interactive platform designed to solve the "I don't know where to start" problem in fitness. Instead of generic gym advice, we help users discover the sports, activities, and movement practices that align with their personality, schedule, and social preferences.

## Core Features

- **The Sport Matcher Quiz:** A dynamic questionnaire that evaluates user preferences (e.g., social vs. solitary, indoor vs. outdoor, high vs. low intensity) to provide tailored recommendations.
- **The Sports Wiki:** A structured database of activities. Each entry includes:
  - **Barrier to Entry:** Estimated initial cost and gear requirements.
  - **Fitness ROI:** Primary muscle groups and health benefits.
  - **The Vibe Check:** A candid look at the community and culture of the sport.
- **Difficulty Filter:** A toggle to view sports by "Beginner," "Intermediate," and "Advanced" entry levels.

## Target Audience

- **The "Gym-Averse":** People who find traditional gym environments intimidating or boring.
- **Beginners:** Individuals looking for a starting point for a healthier lifestyle.
- **Activity Switchers:** Athletes looking to cross-train or try something new.
